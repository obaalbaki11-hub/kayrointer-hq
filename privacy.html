01Who we are

Kayro Interactive ("Kayro", "we", "us") is an independent studio based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We build and run our own products, and we design and build brands, websites, games and software for clients.

We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us any time at omarbaalbaki@kayrointer.com.

02What this policy covers

This policy covers the Kayro Interactive website at kayrointer.com and any conversation you start with us about working together.

It does not cover:

  • The Kayro app at kayrointer.com/app.html. It has its own Kayro App Privacy Policy.
  • Our ventures, such as ROTH (rothbureau.com) and bell2bell (bell2bell.app), which run on their own websites.
  • Websites we build for clients. Each client is responsible for the personal data collected on their own site.

03What we collect

When you browse this website. We don't ask you for anything, and the site doesn't set cookies or use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Like any website, the services that host and deliver it, such as GitHub (which hosts the site) and Google Fonts (which serves our typefaces), receive technical details your browser sends automatically, including your IP address, browser type and the page requested. They handle that information under their own privacy policies.

When you contact us. If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your name, your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us, such as your company, your project and your budget.

When we work together. For client projects, we keep the business contact details, project materials, agreements and invoices needed to deliver the work and run our business.

Please don't send us sensitive personal information (for example health or financial account details) unless a project specifically requires it and we've agreed how it will be handled.

04How we use it

  • To reply to your message and discuss your project.
  • To prepare proposals, scopes and quotes.
  • To deliver, launch and support work we do for you.
  • To send invoices and keep the business records the law requires.
  • To keep this website secure and working.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising or to build marketing profiles.

05Who we share it with

We only share personal information when we need to:

  • Service providers that help us run the studio, such as our email, messaging and hosting providers. Messages you send on WhatsApp are also handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own policy.
  • Professional advisers, such as accountants or lawyers, when they need it to advise us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights.

Some of these providers process data outside the UAE, including in the United States. When that happens, we rely on providers that protect data to recognised standards.

06How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages for as long as we need them to respond and follow up. If we work together, we keep project and billing records for as long as the work continues and for any period the law requires for tax and accounting. After that, we delete the information or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and, for people in the EU or UK, the GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct information that's wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, unless we have to keep it by law.
  • Stop or limit how we use it.

Email omarbaalbaki@kayrointer.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on a request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

08Security

This website is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to client and enquiry information to the people who need it. No system is completely secure. If you think you've found a security problem, please tell us at omarbaalbaki@kayrointer.com.

This website is not aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

09Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the way we handle personal information changes, and change the date at the top. If you're a client and the change affects you, we'll tell you directly.
