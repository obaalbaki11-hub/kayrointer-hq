04 Process

How we work.

Short loops, clear scope, and you see working software early.

  1. 01

    Discovery & scope

    We learn how your business works, agree on what to build and what to leave out, and put a fixed scope and timeline in writing.

  2. 02

    Build

    We design and build in short cycles. You get a live preview link early and give feedback on the real thing, not mockups.

  3. 03

    Launch & iterate

    We ship to production, hand over everything you own, and keep improving it with you once real users arrive.
